# MAH ESTI confidential intake forms

Source forms supplied by Ke:
- **Facial Client Consultation & Skin Analysis** (five pages, client sections 01–10, esthetician-only section 11).
- **Waxing | Client Consultation & Intake** (three pages, client sections 01–07, esthetician-only section 08).

## Client entry points
- Facial: `/mah-esti#consultation`
- Waxing: `/mah-esti/waxing-consultation`
- General booking page also links to both.

These are **consultations, not confirmed appointments or automatic treatment clearance**.

## Confidential storage setup — REQUIRED before accepting responses

Set `MAH_ESTI_INTAKE_KEY` in Vercel's environment variables for Production (and Preview, if needed). It must be a **64-character hexadecimal string representing 32 random bytes**. Generate one locally with:

```bash
node -e "console.log(require('node:crypto').randomBytes(32).toString('hex'))"
```

**Do not commit the key, paste it into client-side source, or share it in a public issue.**
Keep a secure backup of the key. Losing it makes existing encrypted records unreadable; changing it without re-encrypting stored records also makes them unreadable.

Redeploy after setting the variable. Without it, the client can view the intake but cannot submit. Do not bypass this safeguard.

The database model is `MahEstiIntake` and should be generated by `prisma generate`. The forms store AES-256-GCM encrypted payloads, including medical history, allergies, medications, personally identifying information, consent and timestamps. No intake answers are inserted into the ordinary `Inquiry` table.

## Staff
- Inbox: `/admin/mah-esti-intakes`
- Individual record: `/admin/mah-esti-intakes/[id]`
- Authorized access: owner, admin, or an artist linked to Shante.
- Private assessment notes use the correct source form's staff-only fields and are encrypted separately.
- Waxing and facial records are labeled explicitly. The staff member decides whether a service can proceed, be modified, be postponed, or require a medical referral.

## Operating safeguards
- Confirm the studio's privacy notice, data retention/deletion procedures, local legal obligations, staff-role assignments, and breach-response process before going live.
- Test a non-sensitive dummy client submission and staff review in Preview once the key is configured.
- Never put medical details in ordinary email notifications, logs, analytics, or general CRM notes.
- A typed signature records a name and submission timestamp, but is not a certified e-signature service.
- No treatment is automatically approved and no booking time is reserved.
