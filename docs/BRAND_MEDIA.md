## Edit record and owner review

No AI generated or AI retouched image was used. There are no AI before and after comparisons. Derivatives are simple resized WebP exports, one 180 degree orientation correction for the facial image, and real video frames. The source to derivative mapping is the manifest and preparation script.

Before publishing, the owner should review the shortlist and confirm the two Cee video frame choices, plus the MAH ESTI client image used on the homepage. The rejected image is excluded from the public site, including existing seeded gallery records; its original remains in the repository as requested. No database records were overwritten.
