The manifest is `src/lib/brand-media.ts`. `scripts/prepare-brand-media.mjs` creates selected browser ready WebP derivatives during `predev` and `prebuild`; `public/brand/editorial` is generated and ignored by Git. The Cee booking image is a committed frame derivative at `public/brand/curated/cee-braids-feature.webp`, so deployment does not require FFmpeg. Original photographs and footage are never overwritten.

## Edit record and owner review

No AI generated or AI retouched image was used. There are no AI before and after comparisons. Derivatives are simple resized WebP exports, one 180 degree orientation correction for the facial image, and a real Cee video frame. The source to derivative mapping is the manifest and preparation script.

The rejected images are excluded from the public site, including existing seeded gallery records; their originals remain in the repository. No database records were overwritten.
